Key Predictors Explained

Data correlations highlight which factors contribute most significantly to JEE student dropout risk levels:

Daily Study Hours

Severe dropouts correlate heavily with extremely low daily study hours (< 2.5) OR extreme burnouts (> 10 hrs) without proper stress management.

Mental Health challenges

Anxiety and high academic strain without counseling constitute the single largest psychological predictor of dropping out.

Average Mock Scores

Consistent mock test scores act as an indicator of academic confidence. Dropping below 55% average significantly increases dropping out tendencies.

Peer Pressure level

High relative competition levels often create isolation or heavy cognitive fatigue, motivating backup alternative admissions.
